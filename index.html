About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Centre for Astrophysics and Supercomputing (CAS), Swinburne University of Technology, in Melbourne, where I am part of the AGATE team (Analysis of Galaxies At The Extremes). I am also a collaborator of the Institute of Astrophysics and Space Sciences (IA) in Porto, Portugal.

I study the smallest and most dark-matter-dominated galaxies in the Universe. These faint systems are natural laboratories for testing how dark matter behaves and how galaxies form at the lowest masses. I work primarily with integral field spectroscopy, in particular MUSE at the Very Large Telescope and KCWI at the Keck Observatory, where I have taken part in observing proposals and observing runs. I have also worked with other facilities, such as ALMA and UVIT on AstroSat, among others.

At the heart of my work is a simple question: how small can a galaxy be, and what holds it together?
